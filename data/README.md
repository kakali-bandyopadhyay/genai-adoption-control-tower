- **use_cases.json** – 9 GenAI use cases for a fictional hedge-fund technology centre, scored 1–5 on value, feasibility, readiness, differentiation, data sensitivity and model risk, with stage and eligible users. UC02 carries a real offline evaluation baseline from the author's own prototype (labelled).
- **policy.json** – scoring weights, risk-tier cut-offs, sourcing rule, gates per tier, decision window and an illustrative hourly rate.
- **telemetry.csv** – 10 weeks × 5 live use cases, generated by `tools/make_telemetry.py` (deterministic). Planted conditions: UC04 and UC05 meet their gates (expand); UC01 adoption plateaus at 22% (fix adoption); UC02 quality is below the high-tier bar (fix quality); UC03 has a critical control incident in week 8 (pause).
